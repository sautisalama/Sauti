# UX audit and first overhaul pass: 2026-10-31

Method: seeded the dev project, then drove the real app as a brand-new user, an anonymous survivor on a phone, a survivor, a provider, an NGO and an admin, at 1280px and 390px. Screens were captured and measured (`scripts/e2e/audit-ux.mjs`, `audit-signup.mjs`, `audit-flow.mjs`, `audit-verify.mjs`). Functional behaviour was already covered by the UAT suites (see `docs/uat/RESULTS.md`); this pass is about how it feels to use for hours.

## Principles used

| Principle | Where it comes from | What it means here |
|---|---|---|
| Always offer a fast way out | GOV.UK "Exit this page", Refuge, SafeLives, Oomph testing | A visible, non-brand-colour exit; Shift x3; say plainly that it does not clear history. Label it "Exit this page", not "Quick escape". |
| Say what happens next | Empty-state and next-best-action guidance | Every state ends with a single next step, or says "you do not need to do anything". |
| Triage, don't browse | Help-desk triage practice: surface what is waiting and for how long | Lists show who is waiting and since when, in plain words, with the action in the status. |
| Progressive disclosure, about four things above the fold | Dashboard cognitive-load guidance | Next step first, shortcuts second, history third. |
| Low arousal type | Readability, contrast (WCAG AA) | No all-caps labels, 12px minimum, no 900 weights, muted greys that pass 4.5:1. |
| Do not show fake data | Trust | Decorative statistics removed from an admin tool. |

Dribbble and Pinterest pages could not be read in detail through the available tools (search returned tag lists only), so the case-queue and inbox decisions rest on the help-desk and trauma-informed sources above, not on mock-ups.

## What the audit found

1. **No way to leave quickly.** A `SafetyBar` import was commented out in `app/layout.tsx` and the component no longer existed. Nothing in the app let a survivor leave in one action.
2. **Hotline only in the footer.** On the report form the national hotline (1195) sat below the fold.
3. **Floating accessibility button covered form fields** on phones (56px white disc, bottom-left, over the bottom navigation).
4. **Providers saw raw data.** Case cards read `physical`, `Unassigned` and `PENDING`, with no sign of how long the offer had waited or that it needed a response.
5. **Survivor overview did not say what happens next.** "Matches: 0 Found" sat next to a pending report with no explanation. The Matches page then told that survivor to "File a report".
6. **Matches page showed survivor copy to providers.**
7. **Role tiles in onboarding were clickable divs**, not keyboard or screen-reader operable.
8. **Signup gave no password guidance and no route for people who want to stay anonymous.**
9. **Typography fatigue across the signed-in app:** 577 `uppercase`, 358 text sizes below 11px, 333 `font-black`, and several light greys under AA contrast.
10. **An optional integration looked like an error** (red cross for "Calendar not connected", shown on several pages).
11. **Admin "needs your attention today" showed only verifications.** Escalated cases (24 hours with no action) existed only in a once-a-day email.
12. **Admin matching screen showed made-up numbers** (Platform load 42%, Match efficiency 98.2%, Latency 14ms).

## What changed in this pass

| # | Change | Files |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Exit this page" button (amber, outside brand palette) + Shift x3, on report, signup, sign-in and survivor dashboards only | `components/safety/ExitPageButton.tsx`, `app/layout.tsx`, `app/dashboard/layout.tsx` |
| 2 | Hotline strip and "how to leave quickly" note at the top of the report page | `app/report-abuse/page.tsx` |
| 3 | Accessibility button smaller, softer and clear of the bottom nav on phones | `components/a11y/AccessibilityFAB.tsx` |
| 4 | Provider case cards: plain incident name, "Received 12 minutes ago", status "Needs your response" / "In progress" / "Completed" | `components/cases/CaseCard.tsx`, `lib/report-labels.ts` |

## Not done yet, in priority order

1. **Provider triage inbox.** Accept or decline from the list row, a response clock ("12h left before escalation"), sort by urgency then wait. This is the biggest fatigue lever for providers.
2. **Report form in steps.** One question per screen on phones, with voice note first, and a saved draft. Currently one long page of nested selects.
3. **Quiet hours and digesting for notifications**, so providers are not paged for low-urgency items.
4. **Escalation inside the product, not only email.** Notify the provider at 12 hours; show the survivor a calm "we are following this up" message once escalated.
5. **Replace the `.ss-calm` overrides with real components.** The layer is a deliberate stopgap; the long-term fix is to remove the all-caps and tiny-text classes at source.
6. **Admin overview opens in provider mode** for accounts that are both provider and admin; make the landing page follow the last-used mode.
7. **Decide the exit destination** (`EXIT_URL` in `ExitPageButton.tsx`, currently BBC Weather) with survivors from your partner organisations.
8. Remaining items from `docs/uat/KNOWN-GAPS.md` (unencrypted report text, daily-only monitor).
